Design & safety principles

  • One entry per local date. The unit is the day, not the photo. Same-day re-upload replaces the photo and reruns AI; the entry id is stable.
  • Real photos, real daylight. No webcam UI. Users take a deliberate photo with their phone or camera, then upload. Better quality, fewer reflections, calmer flow.
  • Static skin profile is foundational; the journal captures dynamic state. The check-in is intentionally short (≤30s) and never duplicates lifestyle / skin-behavior fields already in Skin Profile.
  • Decision support, not diagnosis. The AI never says "you have X." It says "may", "appears to", "indicators of". Forbidden terms ("diagnose", "treat", "cure") are post-validated and rejected.
  • Deterministic-first insights. Trends, correlations, and referral thresholds are computed in code; the LLM only writes the human summary. Bounds cost and prevents hallucinated correlations.
  • Safety net before AI Suggestions. Reaction → automatic barrier-repair simplification (with snapshot + restore) ships now so the AI Suggestion engine plugs into a working safety net on day one.
  • Photos are not just AI inputs. They build something the user owns and treasures — monthly, quarterly, and yearly progress wrapped with AI captions.
  • Encrypt everything sensitive. Photos are S3 + KMS encrypted; concern/reaction JSONB columns use the same encryptedJsonFieldTransformer family used by Skin Profile. EXIF stripped twice (client + server). OpenAI receives photo bytes only — no PII.
  • Failure preserves the user's work. Analysis errors keep the photo and the ratings; retry is a one-tap action. Cost ceiling queues rather than fails.
  • Calm, supportive voice. No streak shame, no fear-mongering, no medicalised language. Empty states encourage; reaction modals reassure while giving the user agency.
  • Accessibility from day one. Chip-based ratings (not sliders) are keyboard-friendly. Visible focus rings, ARIA labels, screen-reader confirmed. EN + SV i18n at parity.
  • Everything under /journal. /insights, /history, /smart-picks, and /todays-suggestion are reserved for other features and are explicitly not modified in this phase.
